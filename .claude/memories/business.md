### Market
SMBs and agencies seeking automated WhatsApp outreach.

### Stakeholders
- Product Owner (business lead)
- Engineering team
- Sales & Marketing
- Compliance & Legal

### Revenue Model
- Subscription tiers (Free, Pro, Enterprise).
- Pay‑per‑message overage.
- Optional AI premium features.

### Key Metrics
- Leads generated per month.
- Conversion rate (contacted → qualified).
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR).
- AI token usage cost.

### Usage
When planning features, consider impact on revenue streams and metric tracking.
